This screen is for support staff

It lists the companies that have given support staff permission to look at their data, and for how long. It opens only for a person whose sign-in address is on the platform's support list (the PLATFORM_SUPPORT_EMAILS setting), or for a super admin of the platform's own company when PLATFORM_ORG_ID is set.

Are you a customer who wants to let support in or end their access? That is done by your company's owner under Security.

Read-only session

Everything you open is written to that company's audit log. You can look, never change. The session is kept only in this tab and ends when you close it, when the time runs out, or when the company ends the access.
