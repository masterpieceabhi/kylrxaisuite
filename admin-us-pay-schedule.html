This page is for US companies.

States set how often and how soon after the work people must be paid, and the rules differ by state. Confirm your schedule with a US compliance advisor. If you run payroll through a payroll provider, the schedule set up there is the one that actually pays people: keep the two the same.

Add a pay schedule

Pick any real pay date, past or future. Every other pay date is counted from it in steps of one or two weeks.

For a day of the month, type a number from 1 to 31 or the word "last". In a short month, 29, 30 and 31 mean the last day.
