Signing in

How do I sign in?

Type your email or mobile number, then your PIN or password. A right one signs you straight in.

If you are an owner, HR or payroll person, the first time on a new phone or computer you also type a one-time code. After that, that device signs you in with your PIN alone.

I forgot my PIN or password

Press Forgot password or PIN? on the sign-in page. We send you a code by email, text or WhatsApp (the way you chose in My details). Type the code, or tap the one-tap link in the message, then choose a new PIN.

The code did not arrive
  • Check your spam or junk folder.
  • Press Didn't get it? Send it another way under the code box.
  • Codes by text or WhatsApp go only to the mobile number in your My details. Ask HR if it is wrong.
  • A code works for 10 minutes and only once.
Set up an authenticator app (recommended for owners, HR and payroll)
  1. Install Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator on your phone.
  2. Open My details > Authenticator app > Set up.
  3. Scan the QR code with the app, type the 6-digit code it shows, press Turn on.

Next time a code is needed, press Use my authenticator app. Lost your phone? Ask HR or the owner to switch it off for you.

"That did not work. Try again"

The email, number, PIN or password did not match. After 5 tries sign-in stops for 15 minutes; use Forgot password or PIN? to get in with a code instead.

Everyday

Clock in and out

Open Clock in. Your phone may ask to use your location and camera, if your company uses them; allow it, or ask HR why it is needed. Forgot to clock out? Ask for a fix in My hours.

Send my hours

Open My hours, check the week, press Send my hours for approval. You get an email when it is approved or sent back.

Ask for time off

Open Time off, choose the days and the kind of time off, send. Your balance is shown there.

Change my phone, address or bank account

Open My details. Phone and preferred name save at once; legal name, address and bank go to HR to check first. The "Still missing" box at the top shows what is left.

Documents to sign and company policies

Open My documents to sign, and Company policies to read and confirm.

Managers

Approve hours and time off

Open Time for hours and Time off for requests. You see only the people who report to you, and you cannot approve your own requests.

Owners and HR

First set-up

Home shows Getting started with each step: company details and EIN, work location, pay schedule, team, time off policy, handbook and Gusto. Each step ticks itself off when done.

Add people

Add your team: paste a list from a spreadsheet, type names and emails, or share a join link or QR code. Each person gets a set-up link to choose a PIN and fill in their own forms.

Payroll with Gusto

Onn HR does not run payroll. Connect your own Gusto account on Payroll, match your people, and approved hours go to Gusto. Run and approve payroll in Gusto.

Security settings

Security and sign-in: who must type a code on a new device, single sign-on, and support access (our support team sees your data only if you allow it, read-only, for a few hours).

Entrar

¿Cómo entro?

Escriba su correo o número de celular y luego su PIN o contraseña. Si es correcto, entra de inmediato.

Si es dueño, de Recursos Humanos o de nómina, la primera vez en un teléfono o computadora nuevo también escribe un código de una sola vez. Después, ese dispositivo entra solo con su PIN.

Olvidé mi PIN o contraseña

Presione Forgot password or PIN? en la página de entrada. Le enviamos un código por correo, mensaje de texto o WhatsApp. Escriba el código, o toque el enlace del mensaje, y elija un PIN nuevo.

No llegó el código
  • Revise la carpeta de spam o correo no deseado.
  • Presione Didn't get it? Send it another way.
  • Los códigos por texto o WhatsApp van solo al celular que está en Mis datos.
  • Un código sirve 10 minutos y una sola vez.

Todos los días

Marcar entrada y salida

Abra Marcar. Si su empresa lo usa, el teléfono puede pedir su ubicación y cámara.

Enviar mis horas

Abra Mis horas, revise la semana y presione enviar.

Pedir tiempo libre

Abra Tiempo libre, elija los días y envíe.

Cambiar teléfono, dirección o banco

Abra Mis datos. El nombre legal, la dirección y el banco los revisa Recursos Humanos.

Still stuck?

Email support@promptaiusa.com with what you were doing and what the page said. Never send your PIN, password, Social Security number or bank details by email.
