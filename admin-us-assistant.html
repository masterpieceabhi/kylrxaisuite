Ask about your own company in plain English, for example who still needs a W-4 or which onboarding tasks are late. The assistant reads names, departments, job titles, start dates, open checklist tasks, missing forms, time off waiting for a decision and hours waiting for approval. It never sees pay rates, Social Security numbers, bank details, dates of birth, home addresses or health details. Your question and these facts are sent to OpenAI to write the answer. Answers can be wrong: check anything important on its own page. It only reads; it changes nothing.
The HR assistant is not switched on for this server yet. The person who runs the server adds an OpenAI key (see docs/DEPLOY.md). The counts below are live either way.
