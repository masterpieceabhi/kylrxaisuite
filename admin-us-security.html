Single sign-on (SSO)

Let people sign in with your company account (Microsoft Entra, Okta, Google Workspace and others) instead of a password.

First create the OIDC or SAML provider in Firebase Authentication (Identity Platform) and note its provider id, for example oidc.entra or saml.okta. Then enter it here.
Keep at least one break-glass account. If your identity provider has an outage, only those accounts can sign in. Your own email is added automatically when you require SSO.

Support access

If you ask our support team for help, you can let them look at your data, read-only, for a limited time. Every request they make is written to your audit log, and you can switch the access off at any time.
