Workweek, overtime, break and holiday pay rules

Daily overtime, double time, meal and rest breaks, and extra pay for missed breaks depend on your state. Holiday pay is your own company policy. Confirm each setting with a US compliance advisor before you rely on it.

The workweek is a fixed, recurring period of 168 hours (seven days in a row). Overtime is worked out one workweek at a time. Confirm with a US compliance advisor before you change it.

Holiday pay is added for each day marked as a paid holiday on the Company structure page. Holiday hours and extra hours for missed breaks are paid at the regular rate, on top of hours worked, and do not count toward overtime. Salaried exempt staff get neither. Working on a holiday at a higher rate is not calculated.
